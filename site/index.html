How this page works

  1. The updater reads air temperature, relative humidity, wind speed, and the observation time from the Oxford Physics roof station's current-weather page.
  2. It passes those measurements to Neural-UTCI to calculate UTCI.
  3. It passes the same station measurements to pythermalcomfort to calculate the polynomial UTCI approximation.
  4. Each result gets a thermal-stress category based on its UTCI value and the bands below.
  5. Mean radiant temperature (MRT) is assumed to equal air temperature. It is an assumption, not a separate station measurement.
  6. Wind speed is passed through as the station reports it; no adjustment to the model's documented 10 m wind reference is applied.
  7. The latest calculation and its inputs are refreshed daily around midday Oxford time. One snapshot is added to the history each day; older snapshots beyond seven days are removed.

Model input limits

Neural-UTCI accepts air temperature from −50 to 50 °C, MRT from −80 to 120 °C, wind speed from 0.5 to 30.3 m/s, and relative humidity from 5 to 100%. The model is called with its documented oob="clamp" option: values outside those ranges are limited to the nearest accepted value for the calculation. The tables show the original station readings only; MRT is listed as an assumption.

Thermal-stress colour bands

Above 46 °C: extreme heat stress; 38–46: very strong heat stress; 32–38: strong heat stress; 26–32: moderate heat stress; 9–26: no thermal stress; 0–9: slight cold stress; −13–0: moderate cold stress; −27 to −13: strong cold stress; −40 to −27: very strong cold stress; below −40: extreme cold stress. At shared boundaries, the warmer category applies, except that exactly 46 °C is very strong heat stress.

The polynomial calculation uses the original station inputs without clamping and pythermalcomfort's input limits. Its documented valid ranges include air temperature from −50 to 50 °C, wind speed from 0.5 to 17 m/s, and relative humidity from 0 to 100%. If an input is outside the polynomial model's valid range, the polynomial result is shown as unavailable; the Neural-UTCI result is still shown using its own documented clamping behavior.

The station notes that its humidity sensor can briefly report 0% in saturated conditions; those readings are shown as reported and clamped to the model's 5% minimum when calculating UTCI.
